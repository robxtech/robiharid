My Story

My name is Robi Harid, and I’ve spent over a decade leading innovative projects for global brands including TOSHIBA, HSBC, EY, IKEA, and Formula 1. I quickly recognised the transformative power of technology to enhance customer experiences and drive organisational efficiency.

Building on this expertise, I founded RecruitMate AI, an all-in-one platform designed to make recruiters' jobs easier. RecruitMate AI empowers recruiters to streamline candidate communications, saving them hours every day – all while delivering an industry-leading candidate experience.

Hiring: Candidate Experience Made Easy

View Now

After speaking with hundreds of recruiters, I’ve compiled everything I’ve learned into a book designed to help recruiters enhance the candidate experience and build lasting relationships.

‘Hiring: Candidate Management Made Easy’ is a comprehensive guide to creating a recruitment process that attracts, engages, and retains top talent—all while ensuring every applicant feels valued and appreciated.

It explores how treating every candidate with respect and care fosters an exceptional experience, building trust, loyalty, and a reputation for excellence. By prioritising these principles, you’ll position yourself as the recruiter of choice, attracting candidates who choose to work with you time and time again.
